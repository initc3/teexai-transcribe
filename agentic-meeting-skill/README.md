Working product thesis:

The skill should be organized around meeting outcomes, not around a particular video stack. The durable abstraction is a meeting workflow: scheduling, agenda/context preparation, permissioned convening, live capture, transcription, decisions, action items, and follow-up publishing. Jitsi, LiveKit, BigBlueButton, Teams, Zoom, or Google Meet should be treated as interchangeable transports with different control surfaces and permission models.
